Understanding Employee Analytics

These visualizations map workload distribution, checklist compliance, and delivery efficiency across the team. Use them to identify overloaded individuals, track task approval bottlenecks, and ensure program schedules remain on track.

Task Workload

Ranks active employees by volume of Pending / In-Progress tasks. An imbalanced bar chart highlights re-allocation opportunities and potential bottlenecks.

Checklist Breakdown

Stacked chart showing each employee's checklist item states: Completed (green), Awaiting Review (amber), and Pending (slate). A tall amber segment means supervisor review is the current bottleneck.

Completion Efficiency

Ratio of tasks completed on time versus those that lapsed into Overdue. A high on-time efficiency rate indicates healthy planning; persistent overdue tasks suggest under-resourcing.
