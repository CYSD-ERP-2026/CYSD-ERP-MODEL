Understanding Scope & Scale Analytics

This dashboard maps the geographic and operational scope of meetings (Scales) against thematic departments (Domains). By examining this intersection, leadership can visualise whether specific domains are focused on localized execution or engaged in high-level strategic alignment.

Local Execution

Individual & Community scales represent direct localised delivery and team mobilisation. High volumes here show strong field presence and citizen-centric execution.

Regional Operations

District scale indicators show mid-tier execution, regional capacity building, and alignment with regional operations and agencies.

Strategic Alignment

State & National scales track state-wide reviews, national partnership building, and strategic advocacy. This represents system-strengthening efforts.
